## MSA EXCERPTS (reference only)

### Section 6.1 -- Fees & Invoicing

Fees are as set out in the applicable Order Form. Acme issues invoices on
the 2nd day of each calendar month for the prior month's usage, including
any overage above the included event volume (subject to the 5% grace
threshold set out in the Order Form). Invoices are payable net-30 from the
invoice date. Amounts not paid when due, and not under dispute pursuant to
Section 6.3, are placed in past_due status.

### Section 6.3 -- Fee Disputes

If Customer disputes any amount on an invoice in good faith, Customer may
withhold payment of the disputed amount by delivering written notice to
Acme within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date. The notice must identify
the specific amount in dispute and the basis for the dispute. Upon Acme's
receipt of a conforming notice, the disputed amount is under active
good-faith review, and the parties shall work in good faith to resolve the
dispute within thirty (30) days. **No late fees or interest accrue on any
amount under active good-faith dispute during the review period.** Any
undisputed portion of the invoice remains payable in accordance with
Section 6.1. Amounts resolved in Acme's favor become payable within ten
(10) days of resolution.

### Section 9 -- Term & Termination

This Agreement continues for the initial term stated in the Order Form and
renews for successive terms of equal length unless either party gives
written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the current
term ends. Neither party may terminate for convenience during a term.
Either party may terminate for cause upon an uncured material breach by the
other party that remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice
describing the breach. Fees for the committed term are non-refundable
except where Customer terminates for Acme's uncured material breach.

### Section 11.2 -- Data Retention on Termination

Upon termination or expiration, Acme deletes Customer event data and
derived aggregates in accordance with Acme's then-current data retention
policy. On enterprise plans, Acme provides deletion certificates confirming
completion. Customer is responsible for exporting any data it wishes to
retain before the effective date of termination.
