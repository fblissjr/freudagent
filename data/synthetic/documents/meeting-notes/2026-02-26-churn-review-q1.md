# Q1 churn & contraction review -- 2026-02-26

- **Date:** 2026-02-26
- **Facilitator:** carlos.mendes
- **Attendees:** carlos.mendes, aisha.diallo, yuki.tanaka, ingrid.bauer
- **Scope:** two scale-to-growth contractions landing March 1; root causes,
  what we tried, and what we change going forward.

## The two contractions

Both effective 2026-03-01, both scale -> growth, both -$3,300/mo MRR.

- **Cobalt Games (ACCT-1007)** -- scale ($4,500/mo, 60 seats, annual) ->
  growth ($1,200/mo, 24 seats, annual). -$3,300/mo.
- **Halcyon Travel (ACCT-1020)** -- scale ($4,500/mo, 60 seats, monthly) ->
  growth ($1,200/mo, 29 seats, monthly). -$3,300/mo.
- **Combined impact:** -$6,600/mo MRR, roughly -$79.2k ARR.

## Root causes

- **Cobalt:** genuine customer business contraction. Their January content
  launch underperformed and the studio downsized ~40%. Event volume fell
  from scale territory to well under 10M/mo. Nothing we could have done
  differently -- they still love the product, this is budget, not fit or
  satisfaction. Clean, honest downgrade handled well by aisha/carlos.
- **Halcyon:** on us, at least partly. They over-bought scale during the
  2025 peak season and never grew into it -- their usage ran under 20% of
  scale volume for months and nobody flagged it. Post-holiday seasonality
  plus a company-wide cost program forced the conversation, and there was
  fair frustration on their side that we didn't surface the underutilization
  first. We looked reactive.

## Retention plays attempted

- **Cobalt:** none appropriate -- right-sizing was the correct outcome, not
  something to fight. We anchored on re-expansion instead (new title in
  production, possible scale return late 2026).
- **Halcyon:** offered a growth-annual discount to hold some commitment;
  they declined and stayed monthly given the cost program. We did NOT try to
  defend the scale MRR against usage that plainly didn't support it -- that
  would have burned trust we still want.

## Lessons -> actions

- **(a) Quarterly utilization review for all scale accounts.** If we'd been
  watching Halcyon's volume-vs-committed ratio, we'd have opened the
  right-sizing conversation on our terms months earlier. Owner: **carlos**.
- **(b) Product ask: in-app "plan fit" indicator.** A signal in the UI when
  an account is persistently over- or under-provisioned, so both sides see
  it early. Owner: **ingrid** to scope feasibility.
- **(c) Stop counting sales-created seat-expansion placeholders as expansion
  signal.** Both accounts had CRM seat placeholders with no product usage
  behind them, which masked how thin actual engagement was. Those are not an
  expansion signal and should not roll into health scoring.
