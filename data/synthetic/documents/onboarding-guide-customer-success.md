# Customer Success onboarding guide

- **Owner:** carlos.mendes
- **Audience:** new CSMs and AEs covering for CSMs
- **Last updated:** 2026-05-20

## The first 30 days of an account

1. **Kickoff call** within 5 business days of signature. Confirm the
   champion, the executive sponsor, and what report they want on their
   desk that the product must feed.
2. **Integration checkpoint** at day 10: events flowing from at least one
   production source (check `usage_daily` in the account view, not the
   customer's word for it).
3. **First-value review** at day 30: one dashboard the sponsor actually
   opens weekly. If dashboards-viewed is flat, escalate to your lead --
   flat first-month engagement is our strongest churn predictor.

## Plan cheat sheet

starter $299/mo, growth $1,200/mo, scale $4,500/mo, enterprise from
$12,500/mo. Overages have a 5% grace threshold (see the billing FAQ,
KB-133, before promising anything beyond that). SSO is scale+; group-based
role mapping and deletion certificates are enterprise-only.

## Escalation paths

- **Product bug:** support ticket first (so the customer thread exists),
  then the linked engineering issue. Never promise a fix date you didn't
  get from the sprint plan.
- **Billing dispute:** loop in billing engineering via support; no late
  fees accrue during active review, say so explicitly -- it defuses most
  disputes (see the March 2026 replay-window disputes for the playbook).
- **Incident:** support owns customer comms on tickets; you own proactive
  outreach to enterprise sponsors. During INC-2026-0311 the 30-minute
  update cadence on SUP-1042 is what kept Bluewater's CSAT at 4 despite an
  exec-visible outage. That cadence is the standard now.

## QBRs

Quarterly for enterprise, semi-annual for scale. Bring: usage trend vs.
committed volume, support-ticket themes, adoption of features shipped since
last QBR, and one expansion hypothesis backed by product data (not a CRM
placeholder row -- sales-created seat-expansion opportunities are pipeline
bookkeeping, not observed signal).
