ACME ANALYTICS -- DATA RETENTION POLICY
Version 3.2 (approved 2026-02-19)
Owner: security & compliance working group

1. SCOPE
This policy covers customer event data and derived aggregates processed by
Acme Analytics on behalf of customers. It does not cover Acme's own
corporate records.

2. RAW EVENTS
2.1 Hot storage. Raw events are retained in hot storage for 90 days from
    ingestion, where they support reprocessing, dedup-window corrections,
    and incident replay.
2.2 Cold storage. After the hot tier, raw events move to cold object
    storage for a further 305 days.
2.3 Total raw-event retention is therefore 395 days from ingestion, after
    which raw events are permanently deleted on a rolling basis.

3. AGGREGATES
Daily and monthly usage aggregates are retained for 25 months to support
year-over-year reporting, after which they are deleted or irreversibly
de-identified.

4. DELETION ON REQUEST
Customer-initiated deletion requests are honored within 30 days across both
storage tiers and verified by deletion certificates on enterprise plans.

5. BACKUPS
Encrypted backups expire on a 35-day cycle. Data deleted under section 4
ages out of backups within that cycle; backups are never restored in a way
that resurrects deleted customer data.

6. EXCEPTIONS
Legal holds suspend deletion for named accounts only, require sign-off from
counsel, and are reviewed quarterly.
