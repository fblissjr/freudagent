# New-hire onboarding checklist

- **Owner:** Maya Kaplan (VP People)
- **Audience:** People, IT, and hiring managers running a new-hire onboarding
- **Last updated:** 2026-05-02
- **How to use:** copy this template per hire. Boxes are grouped by phase, and
  each line names the owning team. A box is not "done" until the system of
  record shows it.

## Before day 1

**People**

- [ ] Create the **Fernwake People** employee record (this auto-opens a
  **Torchstone Desk** onboarding ticket -- do not open one by hand)
- [ ] Confirm start date, role, and department in Fernwake People
- [ ] Send the welcome packet and first-day logistics

**IT**

- [ ] Image a **Corvid Book 14** laptop for the role
- [ ] Assign an asset tag and record it on the Torchstone Desk onboarding
  ticket
- [ ] Pre-stage SSO app entitlements per the role access matrix (do not
  activate before day 1)

**Manager**

- [ ] Assign an onboarding buddy
- [ ] Write the first-week plan and share it before day 1
- [ ] Book the day-1 welcome and the first 1:1

## Day 1

- [ ] **IT:** issue the badge via **Doorstile**; walk through badge etiquette
  (no tailgating, badge in individually everywhere)
- [ ] **IT:** activate SSO app access per the role matrix (only the apps the
  role needs)
- [ ] **Manager / buddy:** intro the new hire in **Glimmerfen Chat** and add
  them to team channels
- [ ] **Security:** run the security orientation, including the
  **phishing-report button** in the mail client and how to use it
- [ ] **People:** confirm the new hire can log in, badge in, and reach the
  wiki (Bellwether Wiki)

## Week 1

- [ ] **IT:** verify **Bramblehold VPN** connectivity from the new laptop
- [ ] **IT (engineers only):** provision **Marrowgate Vault** access for
  secrets, scoped to the team
- [ ] **People:** expense-policy read-and-acknowledge in Fernwake People
- [ ] **People:** open the benefits enrollment window and confirm the deadline
- [ ] **Manager:** first-week retro 1:1; confirm the buddy relationship is
  working

## 30 / 60 / 90

- [ ] **Manager + hire:** set 30/60/90 goals in **Fernwake People**
- [ ] **Manager:** 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day check-ins on the calendar
- [ ] **People:** confirm security-awareness training completed by day 30
- [ ] **Manager:** 90-day confirmation of role fit recorded in Fernwake People

## Recent example -- the flow working

Most recent clean run: **Talia Reyes (EMP-1107)**, Data Engineer, req
**REQ-2026-08**, start **2026-05-11**. Fernwake People record created the week
prior auto-opened onboarding ticket **IT-2412**; Corvid Book 14 imaged and
asset-tagged against that ticket, badge issued day 1, Marrowgate Vault access
granted in week 1 (engineer). Every phase closed on schedule -- use this as
the reference for what "done" looks like end to end.

Questions or exceptions go to People (Maya Kaplan).
