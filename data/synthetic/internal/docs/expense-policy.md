# Expense policy

- **Owner:** Grace Adeyemi (CFO)
- **Audience:** all Acme Analytics employees and contractors who incur company expenses
- **Last updated:** 2026-01-20

## Purpose

This policy governs how Acme Analytics employees spend company money and get
reimbursed. When a situation is not covered here, spend as if it were your
own money and ask Finance before, not after.

## Filing deadline

All expenses are filed in **Lanternfell Expense** within **30 days** of the
expense date. Claims older than 30 days are reimbursed only with a written
exception from your department head, and habitual late filing is a
performance conversation. Attach an itemized receipt (not a card statement
line) for anything over $25.

## Approval chain

Approval is by amount, and each approver must be above you in the reporting
line:

| Expense total | Approver |
|---------------|----------|
| Up to $500 | Direct manager |
| Up to $5,000 | Department head |
| Above $5,000 | CFO (Grace Adeyemi) |

No self-approval, ever. Splitting one purchase into smaller claims to stay
under an approval threshold is treated as a false claim (see below).

## Corporate card vs. personal reimbursement

- **Corporate card** is the default for travel, lodging, and pre-approved
  business spend. Reconcile every card charge in Lanternfell Expense within
  the 30-day window, with receipts. The card is never used for personal
  purchases, even with intent to repay; misuse is escalated to Finance and
  People.
- **Personal reimbursement** is for incidental out-of-pocket costs when the
  card was not practical. File the same way; reimbursement lands in the next
  payroll cycle after approval.

## Travel

- Book travel through **Wrenfield Travel**, our managed agency. Booking
  outside Wrenfield requires prior department-head approval.
- **Airfare:** economy class. Target cap **$650** for domestic round-trip.
  Fares above the cap need department-head approval before booking.
- **Lodging:** target cap **$220/night** in standard markets. High-cost
  markets (conference cities, major metros) may run higher with prior
  approval noted on the claim.
- **Meals:** per-diem of **$75/day** on travel days, covering all meals.
  Keep receipts. **No alcohol is reimbursed**, on per-diem or itemized
  claims.
- **Ground transport:** rideshare and transit at actuals, with receipts.

## Software and tools (shadow-IT clause)

Any software, SaaS subscription, or online tool purchased for work requires
**IT approval via a Torchstone Desk ticket BEFORE purchase**. This includes
free-trial-to-paid conversions and "I'll expense it myself" tools. Purchases
made without a prior IT ticket are non-reimbursable and are treated as shadow
IT. When in doubt, open the ticket first.

## Non-reimbursable

The following are never reimbursed:

- Alcohol, and entertainment not tied to an approved business purpose.
- Personal travel, upgrades, lounge memberships, and trip extensions.
- Traffic fines, parking tickets, late fees, and personal software or
  home-office furniture without a pre-approved People/IT arrangement.
- Any purchase that skipped a required approval or IT ticket.

## Late and false claims

Late claims are handled as above. **False claims** -- inflated amounts,
duplicated receipts, personal spend disguised as business, or threshold
splitting -- are a serious integrity matter. Consequences range from denied
reimbursement to termination, and may be referred to Legal (Sylvia Ngata).

## Audits

Finance runs **quarterly spot audits** on a random sample of claims across
every department. If your claim is selected you may be asked for additional
documentation; respond within five business days. Audit findings feed back
into this policy. Questions go to Finance or directly to the CFO.
