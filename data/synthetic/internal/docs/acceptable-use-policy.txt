ACME ANALYTICS -- ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
Version 2.1 (approved 2026-02-10)
Owner: Sylvia Ngata (General Counsel), with Security

1. SCOPE AND PRINCIPLE
This policy covers all company equipment, accounts, networks, and data, and
everyone who uses them -- employees, contractors, and vendors. Company
equipment and accounts are provided for business use. Limited, reasonable
personal use is permitted so long as it does not interfere with work,
consume meaningful resources, or violate any part of this policy.

2. AUTHENTICATION
2.1 Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on every account,
    everywhere it is offered. Provisioning is SSO-first through the app
    catalog.
2.2 Administrative and privileged roles must use hardware security keys.
    Software-only factors are not sufficient for admin access.
2.3 Never share credentials, MFA codes, or hardware keys. Each person
    authenticates as themselves.

3. DATA HANDLING
3.1 No customer data on local disks. Work with customer data only inside
    approved systems; do not export it to laptops, personal drives, or
    email.
3.2 Secrets -- API keys, tokens, passwords, connection strings -- live only
    in Marrowgate Vault. Never in source code, Glimmerfen Chat, Torchstone
    Desk tickets, wiki pages, or email. A secret that touches any of those
    is treated as compromised and rotated.
3.3 Classify and store records per the Data Retention Policy; do not keep
    shadow copies.

4. SOFTWARE AND SERVICES
4.1 No personal cloud storage (personal drives, sync tools) for company
    data.
4.2 No unapproved SaaS or software. To adopt a new tool, request it through
    a Torchstone Desk ticket so IT and Security can review it first.
    Unapproved tools are shadow IT and are removed when found.
4.3 Do not disable or evade endpoint security, the VPN, or update
    mechanisms.

5. NETWORK ACCESS
5.1 The Bramblehold VPN is required whenever you access company systems from
    off-network.
5.2 Do not connect company equipment to untrusted networks without the VPN,
    and do not bridge company and personal networks.

6. PHISHING AND REPORTING
6.1 Report suspected phishing using the mail-client report button, or to
    security@acme-analytics.example, or in the Glimmerfen Chat #security
    channel.
6.2 Reporting is never punished, including reporting your own mistake. What
    is punished is clicking a suspicious link or opening a suspect
    attachment and then hiding it. Speed of reporting limits damage.
6.3 Acme runs periodic phishing simulations (campaigns run in February and
    June 2026). Treat every message as if it could be real; report, do not
    click.

7. PHYSICAL ACCESS AND BADGES
7.1 Doorstile badges are personal and non-transferable. Do not lend or
    borrow a badge.
7.2 No tailgating: everyone badges in individually. Do not hold secured
    doors for people you do not know.
7.3 Visitors are signed in and escorted at all times by their host.
7.4 Report a lost or stolen badge to IT immediately so it can be
    deactivated.

8. MONITORING
Company systems, networks, and accounts are monitored and logged for
security and operational purposes (including via Nightledger SIEM). Users
should have no expectation of privacy in company systems. Monitoring is
proportionate and used for security, compliance, and troubleshooting -- not
for casual surveillance.

9. ENFORCEMENT
Violations are handled by People and Legal in proportion to severity, from
coaching to termination, and may involve law enforcement where laws are
broken. Good-faith reports of security concerns are protected, not
penalized.

10. ACKNOWLEDGMENT
Every employee acknowledges this policy annually in Fernwake People, and
again on any material revision. Not acknowledging does not exempt anyone
from the policy.
